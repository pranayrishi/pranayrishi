I’m Rishi (Pranay Rishi Nalem), a high school senior in California who does machine learning research. I study what finetuning changes inside a model, how agents can imagine a screen before they act on it, and how to check the work AI produces. I also build things people use: [SattvaAI](https://sattva-ai-website.vercel.app/), a private agent that runs the apps on your computer, and [Nalem Study Circle](https://nalemstudycircle.org), a free learning platform that has served 2,400+ students in 12 countries.

**Now**

- Leading research on formal verification and LLM theorem proving in Lean 4, in an industry–academic collaboration with Capital One.
- Writing up two world-model manuscripts for computer-use agents (tentatively ICLR 2027) and SpinRC-Bench, a benchmark for spintronic reservoir computing.
- Building SattvaAI.

## Research

| Project | Where | When | What came out of it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Formal verification and LLM theorem proving | Capital One collaboration, research lead | 2026– | Benchmarking proof verifiers for AI-generated math; a generate, verify, iterate loop for proofs in information theory |
| World models for computer-use agents | George Washington University | 2026 | Latent and text-space world models: +0.203 on AgentNet-Ubuntu multi-step prediction, 94% goal grounding, usable horizon from ~4 to ~8 steps |
| Masked diffusion models | Yale University | 2026 | Cutting the training cost of adaptive masked diffusion while keeping any-order decoding |

| CubeSat formation control | Student team, first author | 2026 | Accepted at AAS/AIAA 2026; a model fix cut eight-orbit disagreement from ~182–185 m to 14–20 m |
| Magnon dynamics in CrSBr | UC San Diego | 2026 | Physics-informed ML that classifies magnon propagation and extracts wave equations from video |
| MonoScan | University of Notre Dame | 2026 | Computer vision that finds WSe₂ monolayers in microscopy images, now used in the lab’s pipeline |
| Spintronic reservoir computing | Independent | 2025–26 | 500+ OOMMF simulations; first place at the county science fair |
| Task matrices | Algoverse AI Research | 2025 | One linear map from base to finetuned embeddings; NeurIPS 2025 UniReps workshop poster |
